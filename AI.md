# Use of AI

AI was used. The assistant was [Grok](https://grok.com), by xAI. This file says where, and where it was not.

## Generated pictures and the moving background

The black field, the Latin word "Kagekidan", the thin gold rule, the line-drawn blossom, and the drifting petals were generated with an image model and a video model. A person directed the prompts, rejected frames, and composited the final lockup.

The characters **華撃団** were not drawn by the model. Image models deform kanji. Those three characters were typeset with Noto Serif JP and placed by hand where the monogram K had been. 華撃団 is the troupe's name. It is not a guess at similar-looking characters.

No official illustration, costume, machine, or studio logo was used as a source, and none was asked of the model.

## Music

*さくらさくら* was not composed by a model. The melody is traditional and in the public domain.

`media/theme.mp3` is a new performance. A program synthesizes a plucked-string tone and plays that melody, slowly. The program was written with Grok. Nobody was recorded. No commercial disc was sampled. No music model invented a new tune "in the style of" a living performer.

You can replace the file. Keep the name `theme.mp3`.

## The program

The Windows launcher, its page, the install text, and the wiring around the existing runtime were written with Grok, by xAI, then checked against what the v1.0 build actually does. The Windows port is published by [DiamondDogz](https://github.com/DiamondDogz).

The runtime is not an AI rewrite of Sakura-Wars-PS4RT. It is HekamiAeon's program, built for Windows from the v1.0 line. Credit for that program stays with HekamiAeon and the people named in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

## What AI was not allowed to supply

- The game, or any instruction for obtaining it illegally.
- SEGA's key art or the commercial opening theme.
- A claim that this build includes v1.1 features.
- Invented kanji standing in for 華撃団.
