Windows port of Sakura Wars PS4RT v1.2 (HekamiAeon, `b45cfaa`), with the Kagekidan launcher. Unofficial, not affiliated with SEGA. The game is not included.

New in 1.2.1, with [Crowbar](https://github.com/DiamondDogz/crowbar):
- **F12 screenshots go to the game's Captures** in Crowbar (outside Crowbar, `gpu/screenshots/` as before).
- **Screenshot on every trophy**: with Crowbar's setting on, the runtime saves the game image alone, without overlays, when a trophy unlocks; it shows on that trophy in Crowbar (crowbar-sdk 1.6).

Everything from 1.2: trophies recorded by the game (Achievements tab, overlay notification), Game language option, internal resolutions and experimental ultrawide, FSR 1 and FSR fixes, and with Crowbar the Guide pause, a clean Quit and the trophy test profile.

Keep your progress: saves, trophies and settings are in `out/CUSA16429/` of the previous version's folder; Crowbar carries them over on update.
