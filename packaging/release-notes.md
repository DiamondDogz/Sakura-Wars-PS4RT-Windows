Windows port of Sakura Wars PS4RT v1.2 (HekamiAeon, `b45cfaa`), with the Kagekidan launcher. Unofficial, not affiliated with SEGA. The game is not included.

- **Trophies recorded by the game**, with an Achievements tab and a notification in the overlay (Insert). Kagekidan and Crowbar read them too.
- **Game language** option in the overlay (English, French, German, Spanish), after Apply and restart. Kagekidan's Language choice still works: a new choice there is applied the same way.
- **Internal resolutions** (1024×576 to 4K) and **experimental ultrawide** (21:9, 24:9, 32:9).
- **FSR 1**, and upstream's FSR fixes; the 4K stalls and an NVIDIA startup crash are gone.
- **F12** saves a screenshot.
- With [Crowbar](https://github.com/DiamondDogz/crowbar): the Guide button pauses the game, Quit closes it cleanly, and the trophy test profile can unlock a trophy through the runtime (crowbar-sdk 1.4).

Keep your progress: saves, trophies and settings are in `out/CUSA16429/` of the previous version's folder; Crowbar carries them over on update.
