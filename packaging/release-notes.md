Windows port of Sakura Wars PS4RT (Kagekidan launcher). Unofficial, not affiliated with SEGA. The game is not included.

windows-1.1.4 with a new runtime:

- With [Crowbar](https://github.com/DiamondDogz/crowbar), the Guide button pauses the game (crowbar-sdk 1.2): picture, sound and timers stop while its menu is open, and the controller goes back to the game once released. During a movie, Crowbar says the game cannot pause yet.
- The launcher and everything else are those of windows-1.1.4.

Avec [Crowbar](https://github.com/DiamondDogz/crowbar), le bouton Guide met le jeu en pause (image, son et minuteries arrêtés tant que son menu est ouvert ; pendant une cinématique, Crowbar indique que le jeu ne peut pas encore se mettre en pause). Le lanceur et le reste sont ceux de windows-1.1.4.
